The journey so far

I landed in the U.S. in 2014 to chase a lifelong urge to build things. That took me through a Bachelor's in Civil Engineering at SUNY Buffalo and a Master's at Virginia Tech.

My career took me to sunny Southern California, where I spent a few years specializing in industrial warehouses. Now I've brought that experience (and my sunglasses) to Houston, where I'm focused on a new and equally fascinating challenge: designing material recycling facilities.

I like problems you can stand on. Load paths, foundations, steel and tilt-up concrete are the parts of a building nobody notices when they're done right.
