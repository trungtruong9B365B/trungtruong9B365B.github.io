The journey so far

I came to the U.S. in 2014 to chase a lifelong urge to build things. That took me through a B.S. in Civil Engineering at the University at Buffalo and an M.S. in Structural Engineering at Virginia Tech.

I started out in Southern California at HSA & Associates, designing 300,000–700,000 SF sorting and delivery centers across California, Texas, Washington and Nevada. That meant steel gravity framing, concrete tilt-up walls and moment frames, plus site observations and OAC meetings through construction.

Today I'm a project engineer at JRMA Architects & Engineers in Houston. I'm the technical lead on complete structural calculation packages for modular and prefabricated structures, from concept through state submission. Most of them go to extreme-wind or high-seismic regions.

I hold an active NCEES record, so adding licenses in other states is straightforward.
